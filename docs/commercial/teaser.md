## Tradewind DealFlow

Vertical operating system for professional cash home buyers in Massachusetts and Rhode Island.

**Problem.** Cash buyers assemble generic CRM records, list tools, calculators, and spreadsheets. Those tools do not natively hold source rights, MA/RI equitable-interest and cancellation rules, evidence-ranged underwriting, or hash-bound approvals.

**What exists.** Authorized MassGIS parcel intake, D1 audit and approval ledger, optional RentCast owner enrichment, assessed-only qualification that cannot authorize contact, and Deal Work for conversations, tasks, and ranges.

**What does not exist and must not be implied.** Contracted ARR, national coverage, automated outreach, live calling, formal offers, or closed-deal economics.

**Proof allowed in this draft.** Repository tests and a private synthetic demo. Design-partner proof: none yet.

**Intended conversation.** Asset purchase / tuck-in of a regulated-state acquisitions OS into an investor CRM or property-intelligence platform. Not a SaaS revenue-multiple process.

**Next step after gates pass.** 45-minute technical demo on synthetic data, then NDA and the diligence packet.
