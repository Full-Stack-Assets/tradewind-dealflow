# Design-partner pilot kit

Status: **not run**. No paid operator, no signed outcome, no fabricated lift.  
Commercial targets (hypotheses only): paid audit $2,500; founding partner setup $6,500–$12,500 and $750–$1,500/month, from the approved program roadmap.

This kit is the operating procedure for Track B in the acquisition plan. Completing the documents here is not completing a pilot.

## Why this exists

Corporate development will not open a process on architecture slides. They will ask whether an external MA/RI operator used the product. That evidence must be real and partner-permitted.

## Scope of a founding pilot (when authorized)

- one market (Bristol County, MA and/or Providence County, RI);
- one inbound source (MassGIS standing policy, not cold outreach);
- seller-to-appointment workflow using Deal Work;
- no automated SMS, autodial, formal offers, or closing instructions unless counsel separately authorizes them.

Named profiles in the product design (Tetrault-class, later Moss-class) are **targeting notes**, not signed partners.

## Sequence

1. Paid opportunity audit using `docs/commercial/baseline-metrics-template.md`.
2. Supervised design-partner agreement (private; not in Git).
3. Operator uses Pipeline → Promote → Deal Work on authorized records.
4. Record only events that happened. Empty cells stay empty.
5. If the partner agrees, complete `docs/commercial/outcome-letter-template.md` in the private packet.

## Stop conditions

- Partner will not put PII in an unapproved environment.
- Product would require outreach or offer execution to “show value.”
- Any metric would have to be invented to look complete.
